# Answers to the Assignment Questions

## 1. State how the sentiment analysis has evaluated the review. Is the overall impression positive, negative, or somewhere in between?

The sentiment analysis evaluates the review as mixed, leaning slightly positive. The customer enjoyed the food, especially the ravioli, seafood linguine and veal marsala. However, slow weekend service, high prices, noise and waiting times negatively affected the overall experience.

## 2. Is there anything the restaurant can do to improve the customer experience?

Yes. The restaurant can increase staffing during busy periods, improve its reservation system and reduce noise in the dining area. It could also make outdoor dining more comfortable during colder months and introduce more affordable menu options.

## 3. Is there anything the restaurant is doing that increases negative sentiment?

Yes. Slow service during weekends, high prices and excessive noise when the restaurant is full contribute to negative sentiment. Potential waiting times also discourage spontaneous visits. The high prices make the customer consider the restaurant mainly for special occasions.

## 4. What changes would you recommend for the restaurant?

I would recommend scheduling additional staff during peak hours, improving reservation and table management, installing sound-absorbing materials and introducing weekday promotions or affordable lunch menus. I would also suggest considering suitable outdoor heating and promoting the restaurant's most memorable dishes. These changes could improve customer satisfaction and encourage more frequent visits.
